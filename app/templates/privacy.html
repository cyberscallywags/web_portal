Privacy & Cookies

How Cyber Scallywags handles your data

Last updated: 2026

This is a plain-language summary of how this site uses cookies and local storage. It is a starting template — please review it with the team (and, where needed, a legal advisor) before treating it as your final published policy.

What we store

The Cyber Scallywags portal keeps a small amount of information in your browser to make the site work. We do not currently use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Name Type Purpose
currentUser Local storage (essential) Keeps you signed in and shows your account details.
cookieConsent Local storage (essential) Remembers your choice on this banner so we don't ask again.

Essential vs. non-essential

Essential storage (like keeping you signed in and remembering your consent choice) is required for the site to function and cannot be switched off. Choosing Decline non-essential on the banner means we will not add any optional analytics or marketing storage should we introduce it in future.

Managing your data

  • You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this domain.
  • Signing out removes your stored account information.
  • Most browsers let you block or delete cookies and local storage in their settings.

Contact

Questions about your data or this policy? Get in touch and the Cyber Scallywags team will be happy to help.
